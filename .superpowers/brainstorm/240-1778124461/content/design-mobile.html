Mobile-Specific UX Patterns
Forecast entry — BottomSheet (not inline widget). Slides up from market card tap. YES/NO buttons with haptic feedback.

Daily login claim — BottomSheet auto-shows on app open if streak not claimed today.

Settlement result — Push notification → deep link → /markets/[id] showing SettlementBanner.

Pull-to-refresh — on all list screens (markets, reviews, leaderboard).

Skeleton loaders — on MarketCard, ReviewCard, LeaderboardRow while fetching.
